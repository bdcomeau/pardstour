1 Get it on your phone

BEFORE YOU START — YOU NEED YOUR GOLF CANADA USERNAME AND PASSWORD

Without them Pards cannot find you, cannot fetch a handicap, and cannot set you as Me — so nothing else works. Forgotten them? Reset at golfcanada.ca first.

On an iPhone open Safari, on Android open Chrome, and go to www.pardstour.com. Nothing to download and no app store — Pards runs from the web page.

Add it to your home screen — do this before anything else

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (the square with the arrow, at the bottom) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add. It must be Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot do it.

Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu (top right) → Add to Home screen or Install app → Install.

Open Pards from that icon, not the browser. It runs full screen, it remembers you, and it works out on the course whether or not you have a signal.

2 Set yourself up as Me

You land on the main Pards Tour page with three cards — PARDS GOLF, PARDS EVENTS and PARDS LIVE. Tap PARDS GOLF, then under Players tap Manage Players.

Tap + Add New Player / Search Golf Canada, type your First name and Last name, and tap Find GC ID. Enter your Golf Canada username and password once — they stay on your phone and are used only for handicap lookups. Pick yourself from the results, tap Fetch for your live index, then set your home course and tee deck.

On your own player card tap ✓ This is me (also shown as Set as Me). Pards needs one player marked as you — it tracks your career earnings by it, and it is the only player it can post a score for. Skip it and the app stops you with “One quick thing first!” before your first round.

3 Add the people you play with

Think of this like your contact list on your phone. You need a contact before you can make a call, and Pards needs a saved player before it can build a round. Tap + Add New Player / Search Golf Canada for each partner: search by name to auto-fill his Golf Canada ID and pull his live handicap.

No Golf Canada ID? He still plays — type his name and a handicap index by hand. Refresh All Handicaps updates everyone in one tap.

CHECK FOR THE GREEN LINE BEFORE YOU BET

Under a player’s handicap you should see 🟢 Handicap current as of… with a date. No green line means that number was typed in by hand and has never come from Golf Canada. It will not update on its own. Two phones can then run the same game with two different handicaps for the same man — and the money will not agree at the end. If the line is missing, open his card, add his Golf Canada ID, and tap Refresh All Handicaps.

Buddies and Quick Adds

Open a partner’s card and tap Buddy — Buddies float to the top of every list. Tap Quick Add on up to four players: one tap of the lightning-bolt button on the setup screen drops you and them straight into the slots.

4 Start a round

Choose the course and the tee, then fill the player slots — up to five. Pards carries more than 130 courses already, with ratings and slopes built in, and you can download any other course from Golf Canada.

Pick your betting games (next section), then tap Start Round →.

Tees are filtered for you. Pards shows the decks appropriate to each player, so you are not scrolling past tees you are never going to play.

5 The games you can run

You are not picking one. Tick as many as you want and they all run at once on the same round, each scored and settled separately. There is no limit and no trade-off.

2+ players
Snake 3-Putt Game

A putting penalty game. Any player who 3-putts picks up the snake. Whoever holds the snake at holes 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18 pays every other participant the agreed amount. The snake can change hands multiple times per round — keep your putting sharp.

4+ players
2v2 Nassau (High-Low)

Two teams of two compete across three separate bets — Front 9, Back 9, and Total 18. Each team contributes their low ball and high ball net scores. Auto-presses kick in when a team goes two down. Losing team can invoke The International on hole 18.

2+ players
1v1 Nassau

Head-to-head Nassau between two players. Front 9, Back 9, and Total 18 — three separate bets in one. Run multiple 1v1 matchups simultaneously. Auto-presses and The International available.

3+ players
6-Point Game

A three-player points game played on every hole. The low net score wins 4 points, second place wins 2 points, third place wins 0 points. Ties for low split 3 points each. Points accumulate over 18 holes — highest total wins.

2+ players
Stableford

Points-based scoring against par. Double bogey or worse = 0 pts, Bogey = 1, Par = 2, Birdie = 3, Eagle = 4, Albatross = 5. Higher score wins. Great for keeping everyone engaged even after a rough hole.

2+ players
Skins

Each hole is worth one skin. Win a hole outright and you claim the skin. Tie a hole and the skin carries over to the next. Choose Gross or Net scoring. After 18: void unclaimed skins or Last Hole Sweeps.

4+ players
Vegas

A 2v2 game where each team combines their two scores into a two-digit number, lowest digit first (scores of 4 and 5 become 45). The team with the lower number wins the difference times the bet. The Flip: if a player birdies, the opponent’s digits flip (45 becomes 54). High variance, high drama.

1+ player
Match Cards

Play 1v1 Nassau against players in other groups — without being in the same group. Enter your scores live during the round. Enter your opponents’ scores later in the clubhouse. Full Nassau results calculated at the end.

Birdie Juice runs alongside all of it — the drinks tally, tracked hole by hole and carried into your career record.

6 Scoring as you play

Tap the score for each player on each hole. Scores save as you go — there is no Submit button, and nothing is lost if your phone rings, locks, or drops signal.

Along the bottom of the scoring screen:

ButtonWhat it does
ScorecardsThe full card for every player, front and back nine.
Birdie JuiceThe drinks tally for the round.
SettingsRound settings, Backup & Restore, Help & Info.
End RoundFinish, pause, or abandon — see section 8.
Live LinkShare the round so anyone can watch — see section 7.
Live standings, any time. LIVE STANDINGS and WHO STROKES? sit under the player cards — tap either to see where the money stands and who is getting a shot on the hole you are standing on.

7 The Live Link

Send it once and anyone can watch the scores and every match as it happens, in their own browser — no app to install and no code to type. Nobody has to keep asking you for the standings, or to look at your phone.

The link is view-only. Watchers cannot change a score.

8 Ending a round

You want to…Do this
Finish 18 On the last hole tap ✓ Complete Round. Every game settles itself and the round files to History.
Stop early Tap End Round Early → Save & Pause — after nine holes, after thirteen, or when the rain comes in. It goes to your History tab and you can Resume it whenever you like, exactly where you left off.
Throw it away Abandon Round. The card is gone and nothing is settled.
A paused round is not a lost round. Pards keeps every hole you entered, every bet, and every side game. Resume it tomorrow if you like.

9 Posting to Golf Canada

When the round is complete Pards offers Post Score and asks “Post Score to Golf Canada?” — one tap and it files.

  • It posts hole by hole, not just a total. Golf Canada then applies its own adjustment to each hole for your handicap — a double, a triple or worse is brought down to the maximum you are allowed — so what goes on your record is the score the handicap system wants, not the raw number. You do not adjust anything yourself.
  • Any round from 9 to 18 holes can be posted. Play nine, or thirteen, or seventeen — Golf Canada takes the partial round and files it properly.
  • It can only ever post for you — the player marked as Me. That is Golf Canada’s rule, not the app’s: nobody may post another golfer’s score, so your partners post their own from their own phones.

10 History and your career

The History tab holds every round you have played, newest first. Open one to see the full card, the games, and what each man won or lost. A paused round sits here too, with Resume.

Your record builds up round after round:

WhereWhat it tells you
Career EarningsWhat you are up or down, all time.
Career StatsYour record — and how you have done against each of the men you play with.
Career LedgerRound by round, where the money went.
Career Birdie JuiceThe drinks tally, all time.
Total Rounds PlayedExactly what it says.

You can look at any player’s record, not just your own. Open their card in Manage Players and tap Show Golf Canada Score History — their posted rounds, and a graph of how their handicap index has moved. Every player in the list also carries a small trend line beside their name: green means it is coming down.

11 Back up, or lose it

THERE IS NO ACCOUNT, AND NOTHING SYNCS TO ANOTHER DEVICE

Your rounds, your players and your career earnings live on this phone. Delete the app without a backup and they are gone. There is nowhere to log in and get them back.

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore and tap Export Backup. Do it once after a few rounds, and again before you change phones. To bring it back on a new phone, install Pards, then Import Backup.

12 When something looks wrong

What you seeWhat it means
Two phones, different money Almost always different handicaps or different games. Compare the index for each man on both phones, and check both cards are running the same bets.
No 🟢 green line That handicap was typed by hand and has never come from Golf Canada. See section 3.
“One quick thing first!” Nobody is marked as Me. See section 2.
The app closed mid-round Your round is safe. Phones close apps to free memory — it is not a fault in the app, and it is exactly what Pards saves against. Re-open it and everything through the last hole you entered is where you left it.
A handicap looks stale Pards re-checks a handicap at most once every three hours, and only for players who have a Golf Canada ID on this phone. Tap Refresh All Handicaps to force it.
