What it does, and how to use it

Start here — get it on your phone

On an iPhone open Safari, on Android open Chrome, and go to www.pardstour.com. Nothing to download and no app store.

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share (the square with the arrow) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add. It must be Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot do it.

Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu → Add to Home screen or Install app → Install.

Open Pards from that icon, not the browser. It runs full screen, it remembers you, and it works out on the course.

Courses

Pick it and play. Nothing to set up.

  • 134 courses built in, with men's and ladies' tees, ratings, slopes and stroke index already loaded.
  • Facility information — phone, address, amenities, every tee with its yardages.
  • Download any course in the world that Golf Canada holds, and it is yours, stored on your phone. A download can only ever add a course — it never overwrites one that came with the app.
  • Search any course from the phone.
  • Real-time weather for the course you are playing.
  • Your history at that course before you tee off — best round, average round, career earnings there, your last rounds with scores and the tee deck you played.

Players

Think of it as the contact list on your phone. A contact before a call; a player before a round.

  • Add anyone from Golf Canada by name — it fills in the Golf Canada ID and pulls the live index.
  • No Golf Canada ID? He still plays. Type the name and a handicap by hand.
  • Refresh every handicap at once, or one man at a time.
  • Live handicap on selection — pick a man on the setup screen and his index is fetched there and then.
  • Search, add and delete players; Buddies float to the top; Quick Add puts up to four men in the slots with one tap.
  • Preferred tee deck per player, remembered per course.
  • Typical score for men who play without an index.
  • Optional gender, used only to filter which tees you are shown.
  • Their e-transfer address, so settling up needs no hunting.
  • Their home club, and the date they last played with you.

What you can see about any player — not just yourself

  • Their recent Golf Canada rounds — open any player's card in Manage Players and tap Show Golf Canada Score History to see the scores that are feeding his index, straight from Golf Canada.
  • A handicap trend graph with it — plus a small trend line beside every name in the list, green when the index is coming down.
  • All-time head to head — your record against him, and his against you.
  • Career stats: rounds scored, career earnings, career Birdie Juice, last played.

Your own record — the “Me” card

One player is marked as you. Everything the app tracks, it tracks by that mark.

  • Golf Canada ID · gender · home city (drives your home weather) · home course · your e-transfer address.
  • Your Golf Canada history, in the app.
  • Remove and re-post a recent Golf Canada score without leaving Pards.
  • Choose which betting games appear on the round setup screen — hide the ones you never play.
  • Career stats, career earnings, rounds scored.

Setting up a round

Course, tees, men, games. Then go.

  • 1 to 5 players. Quick Add, or the player dropdown.
  • Tees auto-populate from each man's saved deck, and you can change any man's deck individually — the handicap recalculates itself.
  • Live handicap fetch the moment a name is chosen, plus a manual refresh button beside every handicap.
  • Men's and ladies' tees, filtered per player.
  • Choose your start hole — shotgun starts, or a back-nine tee time.
  • Scorecard-only mode — no games, just the card.
  • Settings & Help right there: money games on/off · Birdie Juice on/off · pinch to zoom · light or dark · the User Manual and Quick Start · Start Over to wipe the setup and begin again.

The games

Tick as many as you like. They all run at once, on the same round, each settled separately.

2–5 PLAYERS
Snake — 3-putt
  • Any player who 3-putts picks up the snake.
  • Six settlements — holes 1–3, 4–6, 7–9, 10–12, 13–15, 16–18. Whoever holds it pays everyone else.
  • $1 · $2 · $5 · $10 · $20 per settlement.
  • Only part of the group wants in? Pick the subset.
4–5 PLAYERS
Nassau 2v2
  • Front 9, Back 9, Total 18 — three bets in one.
  • High-Low and 1-point best low support.
  • Automatic and manual presses.
  • The International on 18 — on or off, standard or Taylor rules.
  • Ball toss with swing-team support that configures the games for you once the toss is done — or set it up entirely by hand.
  • $1 · $2 · $5 · $10 · $20, and every game can be a different amount.
2–5 PLAYERS
Nassau 1v1
  • Everything 2v2 has — presses auto or manual, the International, per-game stakes.
  • Run several 1v1 matches at once inside the same group.
3 PLAYERS, IN A GROUP OF 3–5
6-Point Game
  • Every hole is worth six points: 4 / 2 / 0, and a tie for low splits 3 each.
  • Any combination of three men in the group can be turned on or off.
  • 25¢ · 50¢ · $1 · $2 · $5 · $10 per point, configurable per match.
2–5 PLAYERS
Stableford
  • Default table — 4 under or better 6 · albatross 5 · eagle 4 · birdie 3 · par 2 · bogey 1 · double or worse 0. Completely configurable.
  • $0 (track only) · 50¢ · $1 · $2 · $5 · $10 per point.
2–5 PLAYERS
Skins
  • Per-skin or pot mode.
  • $0 (track only) · $1 · $2 · $5 · $10 · $20.
  • Scoring: Net · half-shot · Gross.
  • Validation on or off. On a tie: carry over, or no carry.
  • After 18: wash the leftovers, or last hole sweeps.
4–5 PLAYERS
Vegas
  • Two scores become a two-digit number, low digit first. Lower number wins the difference, times the bet.
  • Birdie flip — on or off.
  • Ball toss mode. Gross or Net. 25¢ · 50¢ · $1 · $2 · $5 per point, per game.
1+ PLAYER
Match Cards
  • The man chirping you from the range who isn't in your group. “Let's match cards at the end.” This is that.
  • Up to four individual Nassau matches, each with its own challenger, tee deck and stake ($1 · $2 · $5 · $10), auto or manual presses.
  • Each one becomes its own scorecard in your round history. Enter your scores live; enter his in the bar afterwards and the match settles itself.

The scoring screen

One tap per man per hole. That is the whole job.

Across the top

  • The temperature right now, the hole number, the par and the stroke index for the hole you are standing on.
  • On a shotgun start it shows which hole of eighteen you are on, not just the number on the tee block.
  • The full course line — every hole's par, the out, the in and the total.

Every player's row

  • What he is up or down right now, in dollars, across every game running — green or red, on his name.
  • His score to par, and his adjusted handicap with the deck it came from (“8.0 ADJ FROM Deck 2”) — so nobody has to ask why he is getting a shot.
  • Every hole he has played, with his running out, in and total.
  • The score wheel. It opens on his typical score — the one saved on his player card — so most holes are a single tap. It centres on whatever he has now and names every neighbour in golf: ace, eagle, birdie, par, bogey, double, triple, worked out against the par of the hole you are on. Anything from 1 to 15.

Live Standings

One tap opens the money. Every game you are running, totalled across all of them, sorted best to worst — then broken out game by game so you can see which one is carrying you and which one is bleeding. No arithmetic on the back of the card.

Who Strokes?

The question that starts an argument on every second tee. It answers it for the hole you are standing on — the hole's stroke index, then, for each match separately, who gets a shot and whether it is one shot or two. If nobody strokes it says so. It reads off the playing handicaps for that match, so a man can get a shot in the Nassau and not in the skins, and the screen shows both.

Scores save as you go. There is no Submit button. Nothing is lost if the phone rings, locks, runs out of battery or the app closes on you. Open it again and everything through the last hole you entered is exactly where you left it.

The four menus at the foot

Scorecards

Every card in the round, live: the master scorecard and a card for each match game. Share, print or text the whole package from here without waiting for the round to end.

Birdie Juice

The drinks ledger, and it is a proper one. Credits by player, the hole each one was earned on, a Redeem button, and what is left. The count sits on the menu icon so you can see it without opening anything.

The beverage cart drivers love this one. No arguing at the cart about who is owed what — it is on the phone, hole by hole, and it is redeemed with a tap.

Settings — the mid-round gear

This is the part that matters when the day does not go to plan. Mid-round you can:

  • Add or remove a golfer — the man who turns up on the 4th, or the one whose back goes.
  • Change any golfer's tee deck, mid-round, and everything recalculates.
  • Change the starting hole. Shotgun start and you tapped the wrong one? Every score and every game shifts to the right holes and recalculates against the stroke index.
  • Birdie Juice on or off. The International on or off.
  • ⭐ Add any betting game at any time. Start a Snake on the 7th. Add a Nassau on the turn. A 6-Point, a Stableford, a Skins, a Vegas, a Match Card — any of them, any time, mid-round, and the app scores it from the holes already in the book.

End Round

  • Save & Pause — it goes to History and you resume it whenever, exactly where you left off.
  • Abandon — the card is gone and nothing settles.
  • Post a partial round of 9 to 18 holes to Golf Canada.
⭐ Resume a paused round you already posted, and it updates the Golf Canada score — it does not add a second one. Finish the holes, post again, and it replaces the score already posted for that round. The one exception is a round you filed through the Score Centre that came back without a score id — the app cannot update what it cannot find, so it tells you plainly to delete the first one at scg.golfcanada.ca before posting again. It never quietly leaves you with two.

The Live Link

Send it once. Then nobody asks to see your phone again.

Share a link with anyone — the group behind you, the wives at the turn, the four men in the bar who have a side interest in your Nassau. They open it in their own browser and follow the round live: every score, every card, every match as it moves.

  • No app to install. No code to type. No account. It is a link.
  • View only. They can watch everything and change nothing — nobody is putting a number on your card.
  • It carries all of it — the master scorecard and every match game, not a summary.
This is the one that changes the day. The phone stays in your pocket. The people who want to know are already looking.

History and your career

Every round you have ever scored, and what it did to your record.

The top of the screen — your career

  • Career earnings · rounds scored with the app · career record, W–L–T.
  • The Pards Leaderboard — every man you have played with, sorted by money won or lost.

Finding a round

  • Search by player or by course.
  • Filter to rounds that carried a Nassau, 1v1, Stableford, Skins, Vegas, Snake or an International.

Every saved round shows

  • Date · start time · elapsed time · temperature · course name with its logo.
  • Every player and his gross.
  • Money won or lost in that round.
  • Posted to Golf Canada, with the Golf Canada score ID.
  • Rematch · Resume · delete ✕ · and a lock, so you cannot delete it by mistake.

Open a round and you get

  • Won and lost, by player.
  • Collect — an e-transfer QR code and email sharing.
  • Conditions and weather at the start and the end of the round.
  • The master scorecard — gross scores with eagle, birdie, bogey and worse marked, tee decks and yardages.
  • A card for every game — see below.

The individual match game cards

Each game gets its own full breakdown, not a summary:

  • Nassau (2v2 and 1v1) — the front, the back and the 18 settled separately; every press, when it was taken and what it paid; the International if it was called; and the dollar figure each side ended with.
  • Snake — who held it at each of the six settlements, and what changed hands.
  • 6-Point — points hole by hole, the running total, and the money per match.
  • Stableford — points per hole against the table you set, and the total.
  • Skins — which hole each skin was won on, what carried, and how the leftovers were handled.
  • Vegas — the two-digit number per team per hole, the flips, and the running difference.
  • Match Cards — a separate scorecard per challenger, settled once you enter his side.

Sharing and fixing

  • Share or print the whole package — every match card and the master scorecard, in full colour, by print, text or email.
  • Edit a round after the fact. Add a game you forgot to tick, fix a score, change the dollars on one game — save, and everything recalculates and updates your history.

Backup and restore

The one section nobody reads until it is too late.

There is no account, and nothing syncs to another device. Your rounds, your players and your career earnings live on this phone. Delete the app without a backup and there is nowhere to log in and get them back.
  • Export Backup — one file with everything: players, rounds, games, career record.
  • Import Backup — install Pards on the new phone and bring it all back.
  • Do it once after a few rounds, and again before you change phones.

Posting to Golf Canada

  • One tap when the round is complete.
  • It posts hole by hole, not a total — Golf Canada applies its own adjustment to each hole, so what lands on your record is the score the handicap system wants. You adjust nothing.
  • Any round from 9 to 18 holes can be posted.
  • It can only ever post for you. That is Golf Canada's rule, not the app's — your partners post their own.
PART TWO

Getting started

Five minutes, once. Then you never do it again.

Set yourself up as Me

You land on the main Pards Tour page with three cards — PARDS GOLF, PARDS EVENTS and PARDS LIVE. Tap PARDS GOLF, then under Players tap Manage Players.

Tap + Add New Player / Search Golf Canada, type your First name and Last name, and tap Find GC ID. Enter your Golf Canada username and password once — they stay on your phone and are used only for handicap lookups. Pick yourself from the results, tap Fetch for your live index, then set your home course and tee deck.

On your own player card tap ✓ This is me (also shown as Set as Me). Pards needs one player marked as you — it tracks your career earnings by it, and it is the only player it can post a score for. Skip it and the app stops you with “One quick thing first!” before your first round.

Add the people you play with

Think of this like your contact list on your phone. You need a contact before you can make a call, and Pards needs a saved player before it can build a round. Tap + Add New Player / Search Golf Canada for each partner: search by name to auto-fill his Golf Canada ID and pull his live handicap.

No Golf Canada ID? He still plays — type his name and a handicap index by hand. Refresh All Handicaps updates everyone in one tap.

CHECK FOR THE GREEN LINE BEFORE YOU BET

Under a player’s handicap you should see 🟢 Handicap current as of… with a date. No green line means that number was typed in by hand and has never come from Golf Canada. It will not update on its own. Two phones can then run the same game with two different handicaps for the same man — and the money will not agree at the end. If the line is missing, open his card, add his Golf Canada ID, and tap Refresh All Handicaps.

Buddies and Quick Adds

Open a partner’s card and tap Buddy — Buddies float to the top of every list. Tap Quick Add on up to four players: one tap of the lightning-bolt button on the setup screen drops you and them straight into the slots.

Start a round

Choose the course and the tee, then fill the player slots — up to five. Pards carries more than 130 courses already, with ratings and slopes built in, and you can download any other course from Golf Canada.

Pick your betting games (next section), then tap Start Round →.

Tees are filtered for you. Pards shows the decks appropriate to each player, so you are not scrolling past tees you are never going to play.

When something looks wrong

What you seeWhat it means
Two phones, different money Almost always different handicaps or different games. Compare the index for each man on both phones, and check both cards are running the same bets.
No 🟢 green line That handicap was typed by hand and has never come from Golf Canada. See Getting started, above.
“One quick thing first!” Nobody is marked as Me. See Getting started, above.
The app closed mid-round Your round is safe. Phones close apps to free memory — it is not a fault in the app, and it is exactly what Pards saves against. Re-open it and everything through the last hole you entered is where you left it.
A handicap looks stale Pards re-checks a handicap at most once every three hours, and only for players who have a Golf Canada ID on this phone. Tap Refresh All Handicaps to force it.
